# Private notation

- **Scope:** global
- **Surfaced in:** theoretical RL
- **Protocol version:** v0.2
- **Rule it motivated:** Closing block capped at 70 words; session-local labels only as parenthetical scroll-back aids; standing items kept in one file.

## What happened
Per-message summary blocks referred to session-local labels (`Lemma O1`, `ledger row L4`, `Q3`)
plus a replayed list of standing open items. Returning after several days, the researcher could
not decode them without scrolling back through the session.

## Why it was a problem
It is the protocol's own failure mode: a summary whose decoding cost is externalized onto the
reader. Content correct, artifact unusable.

## Fix
Closing block capped at 70 words; session-local labels appear only as parenthetical scroll-back
aids attached to a self-contained description; standing items are kept in one maintained file and
referenced by count and delta, never replayed.
