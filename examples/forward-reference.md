# Forward reference

- **Scope:** global
- **Surfaced in:** theoretical RL
- **Protocol version:** v0.1
- **Rule it motivated:** No symbol used before it is defined; motivation precedes machinery.

## What happened
A notation table, a clipping operation, and a threshold were introduced before the reader had any
reason to care about them. The researcher's note at that point: "I have no idea what these are,
why clipping was mentioned."

## Why it was a problem
It forces the reader to hold unexplained machinery in working memory while evaluating something
else, which is exactly how a reader stops checking and starts skimming.

## Fix
No symbol is used before it is defined; motivation precedes machinery.
